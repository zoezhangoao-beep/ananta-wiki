Starter Guide Pre-Launch Overview

ANANTA Beginner's Guide

An English starter guide built exclusively from official ANANTA announcements — introducing the game's urban premise, confirmed organizations, featured characters, and unannounced systems labeled To be confirmed.

Premise & Release Organizations & Roles Confirmed Characters To Be Confirmed

1. What Is ANANTA?

Officially Confirmed

ANANTA is an upcoming free-to-play Urban Open World RPG developed by Naked Rain and published by NetEase Games for PC, PlayStation 5, and mobile platforms (App Store and Google Play), with a global release announced for January 15, 2027.

1.1 Dual-City Setting

Official materials describe the story unfolding between Nova City and Chongxiao (including Lingyun City), where everyday city life meets unexpected threats.

1.2 Task Force Premise

Players arrive in Nova City as the new captain of a special task force, assembling a team from eccentric characters and taking on distinct roles across urban life.

Sources: anantagame.com/en/ · NetEase Games Official Announcement (2025-09-22) · Launch Date Trailer

2. Confirmed Organizations & Playable Roles

Officially Confirmed

Official trailer descriptions and character pages introduce several organizations and role-switching paths across the city:

  • ACD: The task force led by the Captain, as referenced on the official website (the full expansion of the acronym ACD is not confirmed in the official English sources and is left unexpanded).
  • Organizations Mentioned in Missions: The official gameplay trailer states players carry out missions for organizations including the NCAA, DK9, and Cat Express.
  • City Roles: Official NetEase news confirms switching between diverse urban roles including a cop, hacker, delivery man, and live-streamer (alongside department head and well-to-do elite mentioned in the trailer).
Sources: anantagame.com/en/ · Gameplay Trailer (WhuqYZnHkW0) · NetEase Games Official Announcement

3. Featured Confirmed Characters

Officially Confirmed

The homepage Featured Characters section highlights three characters directly verified on the official global website:

  • Captain: Captain of the ACD task force ("Traffic jam? Time to fly.").
  • Taffy: Joined the ACD expecting easy hours, now delivering bizarre cargo on her bike, and a founding member of the Yeet Squad ("Ugh, work stinks! Never shoulda taken this job.").
  • Richie: Described as a major boon to the ACD team who fights crime by day and moonlights at night ("It's a shot in the dark, but I gotta go with my gut.").
Source: anantagame.com/en/

4. Systems Awaiting Official Confirmation

To Be Confirmed
