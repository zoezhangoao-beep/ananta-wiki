World & Traversal Pre-Launch Overview

ANANTA Exploration Guide

Verified exploration details covering ANANTA's announced cities, vertical environment layers, swinging traversal, and official vehicle lineup — with unconfirmed map mechanics labeled To be confirmed.

Cities & Districts Swinging & 3C Vehicles & City Life To Be Confirmed

1. Announced Cities & Vertical World Layers

Officially Confirmed

Official website pages and NetEase's Tokyo Game Show announcement describe a dual-city adventure with multi-layered indoor and outdoor exploration:

1.1 Nova City & Chongxiao

  • Nova City: Features Downtown and the Veluxity Zone alongside bustling business districts and hidden alleyways.
  • Chongxiao (Lingyun): Features Weilan Lake, Mingpu District, and Longqi Village highlighted on the official global website.

1.2 Vertical Exploration Layers

Indoor and outdoor scene interactions span three vertical layers across the city: rooftops, mid-levels, and ground floors.

Sources: anantagame.com/en/ · NetEase Games Official Announcement · Launch Date Trailer

2. 3C System & Swinging Traversal

Officially Confirmed

NetEase Games highlights unrestrained city movement built around its 3C (Character, Camera, Control) system:

  • Free Movement & Swinging: Players can reach visible destinations across the city using special traversal techniques including swinging.
  • On-Foot & Unconventional Means: Players explore neighborhoods, business districts, and alleyways on foot as well as unconventional traversal methods.
Source: NetEase Games Official Announcement (2025-09-22)

3. Official Vehicles & City Interactions

Officially Confirmed

Official announcements list specific vehicles and interactive hotspots scattered across the city:

  • Confirmed Vehicles: Cars, taxis, buses, subways, and helicopters — usable both for cruising maps and completing missions.
  • Interactive City Life: Map hotspots trigger events like petting cats in parks and gang clashes, alongside an NPC ecosystem, snooping on passersby's phones, and street racing.
Source: NetEase Games Official Announcement (2025-09-22)

4. Unconfirmed Map & Traversal Mechanics

To Be Confirmed

Specific player controls, UI menus, and traversal limits have not yet been detailed in official manuals.

To be confirmed: Keybinds and controller layouts, fast-travel mechanics, full district boundaries, vehicle summoning or ownership rules, swinging stamina or cooldowns, and collectible tracking systems.

Quick Facts

  • Cities: Nova City & Chongxiao (Lingyun)
  • Traversal: 3C System & Swinging
  • Vehicles: Cars, Taxis, Buses, Subways, Helicopters
  • Verticality: Rooftops, Mid-levels, Ground Floors
  • Fast Travel & Controls: To be confirmed
