Battle Basics Pre-Launch Overview

ANANTA Combat Guide

A factual overview of ANANTA's combat mechanics as explicitly confirmed in NetEase Games' official announcement and gameplay trailers — with unannounced classes, skills, and formulas marked To be confirmed.

Environmental Combat Disarming & Counters Team & Role Switching To Be Confirmed

1. Dynamic Environmental Combat & Props

Officially Confirmed

According to NetEase Games' official announcement, ANANTA's combat emphasizes creative freedom and interactive city terrain inspired by action-movie choreography:

1.1 Weaponizing Urban Props

Smooth, fast-paced encounters allow players to fight using items across the environment — explicitly including golf clubs and trash bins — to defeat enemies.

1.2 Interactive City Terrain

Battles make dynamic use of interactive urban terrain directly inside city streets and interiors rather than isolated static arenas.

Source: NetEase Games Official Announcement (2025-09-22)

2. Disarming Enemies & Counterattacking

Officially Confirmed

Official combat descriptions highlight tactical flexibility and defensive timing during fast-paced skirmishes:

  • Disarming & Enemy Weapons: Beyond their own gear, players can disarm foes and take enemy weapons during battle to experience different combat styles.
  • Dodge, Block & Counterattack: Multi-mechanic combat supports precise dodging and blocking to trigger counterattacks.
Source: NetEase Games Official Announcement (2025-09-22)

3. Team Assembly & Role Switching

Officially Confirmed

Both the official website and gameplay trailer emphasize assembling a squad and playing as varied characters across Nova City and Chongxiao:

  • Assembling a Squad: Recruit and play as distinct characters introduced on Featured Characters, including Captain, Taffy, and Richie.
  • Promotional Footage Highlights: Official trailers show seamless transitions between city exploration and varied hand-to-hand, ranged, and improvised encounters.
Sources: anantagame.com/en/ · Gameplay Trailer (WhuqYZnHkW0) · Launch Date Trailer

4. Classes, Stats & Formulas Pending Confirmation

To Be Confirmed

Numerical stats, character classes, and progression balance have not been officially disclosed.

To be confirmed: Character class or archetype labels, elemental/attribute interactions, weapon durability and damage statistics, skill cooldowns, parry/dodge frame timings, and enemy/boss mechanics.

Quick Facts

  • Props Confirmed: Golf Clubs & Trash Bins
  • Tactics: Disarm Foes & Take Enemy Weapons
  • Defense: Precise Dodging, Blocking, Counterattack
  • Classes, Stats & Formulas: To be confirmed
